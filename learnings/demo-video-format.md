# Demo Video Format: Live, Slide, or Hybrid

For B2B SaaS/fintech product demos, the format decision — live screen recording vs slide deck vs hybrid — has a clear research-backed answer.

## The short version

**Live screen recording wins for B2B SaaS proof. Hybrid (live + strategic cutaways) is the winning format in practice. Pure slide-deck style doesn't work for fintech product evaluation.**

## Why live recordings outperform animated/slide alternatives

- **Real product UI builds trust.** Showing the real interface builds more credibility than animated explainers for most SaaS products. Sophisticated buyers (PMs, CIOs, analysts) distrust marketing-polished content — they want to see the product do the thing.
- **For onboarding/instructional content, clarity matters more than polish.** A simple recording with clear narration beats a highly-produced animated video.
- **Top-performing B2B demos convert better when they show the product in action** rather than describing it.

## Hybrid is the dominant format in 2026

Modern SaaS teams combine formats across the funnel:
- **Top-of-funnel:** explainer or UI demo for attraction
- **Middle:** screencast + animation for showing concrete capabilities
- **Bottom:** interactive walkthrough for those in trial

But within a single demo video, the pattern is **live-dominant with strategic cutaways**, not one-or-the-other. The research consensus: real screen recordings + strategic animation + clear narration outperform pure slide-deck or pure animated formats.

## When cutaways (b-roll) earn their place

B-roll is secondary footage intercut with primary footage to add context, cover edits, establish place, or reinforce a key point. In product demos, appropriate cutaways include:
- **Opening shots for context** (e.g. the brand logo or a scene-setting graphic)
- **Problem/context cutaways** showing the workflow that needs help
- **Product close-ups** zooming in on a specific feature
- **Infographic / stat card moments** to emphasize a data point that's hard to read on the live UI

**Timing:** a single b-roll clip typically runs 2–10 seconds. Long enough to land a point, short enough not to overstay. For interviews, the recommendation is 10–15 cutaways per minute; for product demos the ratio is lower because live screen is carrying the narrative.

## Why pure slide-deck style doesn't work for fintech product proof

1. **The product is the proof.** For Parallax specifically: the differentiator is "eight parallel API calls vs sequential." A slide saying "Parallax runs 8 parallel calls" is the same claim stripped of evidence. For a PM/CIO audience evaluating tooling, that's a downgrade.
2. **Slide-style reads as pitch deck, not product demo.** Fintech buyers specifically distrust pitch-looking content.
3. **Slides throw away the most expensive-to-produce asset** (the live screen recording).
4. Videos that do lean slide-heavy in fintech are usually for retail audiences or top-of-funnel landing pages — not for serious buyer evaluation.

## Duration benchmarks

- **Top-of-funnel engagement:** 60–120 seconds
- **Deeper product walkthroughs:** up to 3 minutes
- **Top-performing demos:** 10–12 steps, 80%+ completion rate
- **First 7 seconds are disproportionately important** for retention — hook has to land immediately

V1's ~1:40 voiced length sits correctly in the middle-funnel walkthrough zone.

## Applied to Parallax

**Format:** live-dominant picture-in-picture (HeyGen's Product Demo template, not Weather Report). Small avatar overlay, the Parallax/Cowork recording fills the frame.

**Proposed cutaway treatment for V1:**

| Timestamp | Mode | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:14 | Live, avatar PiP | Hook needs to feel real — plain-English prompt, typed by a human |
| 0:14–0:25 | Live, hero framing (avatar can shrink/move) | First parallel calls firing. This IS the differentiator shot. Cutaway here would kill the "sequential models can't do this" proof |
| 0:25–1:45 | Live, speed-ramped — optional 5–8s cutaway to animated diagram of 8 parallel calls | Long tool-call stretch. Ramped in the local pipeline. A clean animated diagram during this stretch could elevate production without losing the live frame |
| 1:20 (ICIR vault callout) | **Cutaway to branded stat card, ~4s** | Credibility beat. A static brand card reads cleaner than a lower-third over busy UI |
| 1:52–2:14 | Live, zooms/highlights | Viewer needs to read the brief. Trajectory paragraph at 2:00 benefits from zoom overlay, not cutaway |
| End card | Branded wordmark | "PARALLAX. Solve the market." always as a cutaway |

**HeyGen template implication:** the product-demo PiP template handles the live portion. Full-frame cutaways during the live body may require splicing with local ffmpeg post-HeyGen-render, unless HeyGen's scene-by-scene feature supports mid-video cutaways natively (to verify).
